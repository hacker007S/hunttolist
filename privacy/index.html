HuntGo for eBay — Privacy Policy

Pycode Ltd · Effective 28 August 2026 · Applies to the Chrome extension "HuntGo for eBay" version 3.9 and later · updated 31 August 2026

HuntGo for eBay runs inside your browser. Pycode Ltd operates no server for it and receives none of the data it handles. This page explains what the extension reads, where it sends it, and what it stores.

What the extension reads

  • On pages you open on amazon.co.uk, aliexpress.com and ebay.co.uk, and on pages it opens for you in background tabs during a hunt or validation run you started or scheduled: product listing data — titles, prices, sold or order counts, ratings, images and, on eBay search results, seller usernames and feedback counts.
  • On your own eBay Seller Hub Product Research (Terapeak) page: the results figures (sell-through, sold counts, sellers, prices).
  • If you turn on "Show the HuntGo panel on my pipeline sheet" in Settings, the extension also runs on your own pipeline Google Sheet, where it only draws its control panel; it reads no cell content and does not run on any other spreadsheet.
  • It does not read cookies, passwords, payment details, messages or any page outside those sites. It relies on the sites' existing logins in your browser and never signs in for you.

Where data goes

  • Your Google Sheet. When you press Send or Add, or start a run, the products and the verdicts the extension computes are posted to a Google Apps Script web app that you deploy on your Google Sheet, at the URL you paste into Settings. There is no built-in destination. The web app is reachable by anyone who has its URL unless you set the optional pipeline token, so keep the URL private and set a token.
  • AI providers, only if you add your own key. If you enter a Google Gemini or Anthropic API key in Settings, product titles and your recent accepted keywords are sent to that provider to choose search keywords, and, when finding suppliers, the source listing image and up to eight candidate supplier images are sent to Gemini for matching. This happens under your own account with that provider.
  • Licence check. If you enter a HuntGo Pro licence key, the key and a device label are sent to Lemon Squeezy (our payment and licensing provider) to confirm the subscription is active. Payment itself happens on Lemon Squeezy's site under its own privacy policy; the extension never sees your card details.
  • Email. If you set an alert address on your sheet's Settings tab, winner alerts and the weekly summary are sent from your own Google account to that address by your own Apps Script.

What is stored on your device

Settings, screening criteria, keyword history, daily usage counters, the free-plan counters, your licence status, and (if entered) your API keys and pipeline token are stored in Chrome's extension storage on your device only. They are not synced and are not sent to Pycode Ltd. Removing the extension deletes all of it; your sheet stays with you.

What the extension does not do

  • No analytics, tracking or advertising identifiers.
  • No selling, sharing or transfer of data to third parties beyond the services you configure above.
  • No captcha solving. If a marketplace shows a captcha, the run pauses and asks you to handle it.
  • No creating, editing or publishing of eBay listings.

Limited use

Data obtained through the extension is used only to provide the product research features you see in the extension and is not used for any other purpose, in line with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

  • Change or remove the pipeline URL, token and API keys at any time in Settings.
  • Turn the scheduled morning hunt off in Settings (it is off by default).
  • Uninstall the extension to delete everything it stored in Chrome.
